Jordan is its people.

Jordan feels human before it feels monumental. Beyond Petra and Wadi Rum, the country is shaped by hospitality, family life, coffee rituals, local dress, shared meals and communities that still carry strong regional identity. Travel safety

Help should be one tap away.

If something goes wrong, you should not have to search for help. Here is the emergency number and the simple habits that keep a trip safe.

In any emergency
911

911 is Jordan’s unified emergency number: it reaches police, ambulance and fire. It works day and night from any phone.

Call 911 now

Good habits on the road

Before a hike

Check reserve or trail guidance, carry water, and respect route closures in Dana, Mujib and Ajloun.

Desert travel

Use a recognised guide or operator for remote Wadi Rum routes and overnight camps.

Religious sites

Dress modestly and follow local photography and access rules at churches, mosques and shrines.

About us

Sawsana helps you understand Jordan before you travel through it.

Sawsana is a Jordan tourism website designed to make planning feel easy, inspiring and personal. It brings together destinations, food, culture, travel tools, events and a smart trip planner in one place — so instead of jumping between tabs, you discover Jordan through one clear experience.

It is useful for visitors who want more than a list of places. Sawsana helps you choose what fits your mood, understand what makes each region special, and leave with a route you can actually use.

What Sawsana is

A travel companion for Jordan: part guide, part planner and part cultural introduction. We built it to help visitors move from curiosity to a real itinerary — with beauty, clarity and respect for place.
